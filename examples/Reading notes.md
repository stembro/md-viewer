## Your files stay yours

The viewer reads documents directly from your computer. Raw HTML and scripts stay inactive, and remote images are not downloaded.

Local images work too, including paths with spaces:

![A simple illustration of layered green hills](assets/green%20hills.svg)

## Take your time

Read, search, zoom, print. Use your favorite editor to make changes; the viewer will refresh automatically and keep your scroll position.
